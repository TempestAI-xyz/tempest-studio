Status decision ladder:
- answered = explicit latest-applicable user requirement.
- assumed = conservative default for ambiguity that does not materially change product risk or scope.
- open = unanswered decision that changes trust, safety, legality, money, identity, privacy, irreversible data changes, external processing, physical behavior, hardware/platform feasibility, extreme scale, or launch feasibility.

Do not open ordinary preferences, nice-to-have implementation details, color/tone choices, or questions where the safest answer is obvious.
Translate impossible guarantees such as "never lose data", "instant", "secure", or "cheap and complete" into practical measurable requirements. Open only if the remaining tradeoff is a real user decision.
For broad requests, keep first release and future expansion separate.
For every open item, include requirement text, question, recommendation, and reason.
Self-check once for missing important requirements, missed true blockers, over-opened trivial questions, duplicates, and stale contradictions.
