Return the full current requirements list, not only deltas.

Build one current list from the latest applicable request:
- Include explicit wants, constraints, exclusions, audience, workflows, data, platform, integrations, style, scale, deadline, and later/future items that matter.
- Add practical inferred requirements only when they make the product coherent.
- Remove stale or conflicting requirements after pivots or corrections.
- Avoid duplicate requirements and implementation-plan detail.

Statuses:
- answered = explicit latest-applicable user requirement or exclusion.
- assumed = safe recommendation for non-blocking ambiguity.
- open = true blocker where a reasonable assumption could create the wrong product, unsafe trust boundary, unresolved contradiction, or major architecture/scope choice.

Use open sparingly. Strong open candidates include contradictions; sensitive/private/regulated data; identity, roles, permissions, and accountability; external processing of private content; money, payouts, prizes, taxes, or legal eligibility; destructive bulk operations; physical or life-safety behavior; hard platform or hardware targets; extreme scale; impossible guarantees; and tight deadline/cost scope conflicts.
For impossible guarantees, convert to practical reliability/recovery requirements and open only if the risk boundary remains a real user decision.
For broad messy prompts, distinguish first-release scope from future compatibility.

For every open item, include requirement text, question, recommendation, and reason.
Before returning, self-check for missing major requirements, contradiction pairs, stale items, duplicate bloat, missed true blockers, and unnecessary blockers.
Do not produce Phase 2 implementation plans.
